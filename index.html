About the Project

This project develops a digital display advertising campaign for Morning Bell Coffee Roasters. The campaign focuses on brand awareness by introducing the brand through coffee-focused visuals, consistent messaging, and a dedicated landing page.

Semester Executive Summary

Throughout the semester, this project will bring together campaign strategy, audience targeting, advertising design, benchmark estimates, and website development for Morning Bell Coffee Roasters.

The campaign includes four display ad versions and a dedicated landing page designed to maintain continuity between the ads and the website. This summary will be expanded with additional project work, evaluation, and results as the semester progresses.
