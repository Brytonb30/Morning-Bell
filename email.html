Growing Our Community

More Than Just Coffee

We want to connect with coffee lovers wherever they are. Here's how Morning Bell will grow its email community and keep subscribers coming back.

01

In-Store Customers

Where & How We Collect:
QR codes at checkout, on tables, and on printed receipts. Baristas can also invite customers to join. In-store signs will promote the email list.

Immediate Value:
A 10% discount on their first bag of freshly roasted coffee.

Long-Term Value:
Subscribers receive seasonal drink announcements, new roast releases, and exclusive customer offers.

02

Iowa State Students

Where & How We Collect:
QR code flyers around campus, Instagram promotions, and social media giveaways. Organic posts and targeted social ads will encourage students to sign up.

Immediate Value:
A digital coupon for a discounted coffee or a free drink upgrade on their next visit.

Long-Term Value:
Student-focused promotions, seasonal drinks, campus updates, and local event announcements.

03

Online Shoppers

Where & How We Collect:
An optional email sign-up during online checkout and a sign-up prompt on the Morning Bell website. Social media links will also direct visitors to the form.

Immediate Value:
A 10% discount on their first online coffee order.

Long-Term Value:
New coffee releases, brewing tips, coffee origin stories, and exclusive online ordering offers.
