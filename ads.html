Behind the Campaign

Campaign Overview

Learn about the goal, message, audience, and strategy behind the Morning Bell Coffee Roasters campaign.

Campaign Goal

The primary goal of this campaign is to increase brand awareness for Morning Bell Coffee Roasters. The digital display ads are designed to introduce the brand to potential customers, build recognition, and encourage people to learn more about its coffee.

Target Audience

Morning Bell Coffee Roasters primarily targets college students ages 18–24 in Ames, Iowa, especially Iowa State University students who enjoy coffee, need convenient places to study or socialize, and are looking for quality coffee at an affordable price. The secondary target audience includes young professionals and local residents who value freshly roasted coffee, a welcoming café atmosphere, and community events. The campaign connects with these audiences through engaging digital ads, appealing visuals, convenience, and a memorable message that encourages customers to discover Morning Bell Coffee Roasters.

Campaign Hook

“Fresh Roasted. Made for Your Morning.”

This message connects Morning Bell Coffee Roasters with the everyday coffee routine. It introduces the brand in a simple, memorable way and encourages people to explore what it offers.

Call to Action

Discover Our Coffee encourages viewers to click an ad and visit the landing page to learn more about Morning Bell Coffee Roasters.

Ad Placements

The campaign uses four digital display ad formats: Medium Rectangle (300 × 250 px), Leaderboard (728 × 90 px), Wide Skyscraper (160 × 600 px), and Half Page (300 × 600 px). Each format offers a different way to present the campaign message while directing viewers to the same landing page.
