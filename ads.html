Behind the Campaign

Campaign Overview

Learn about the goal, message, audience, and strategy behind the Morning Bell Coffee Roasters campaign.

Campaign Goal

The primary goal is to increase brand awareness for Morning Bell Coffee Roasters. The display ads introduce the brand to potential customers, build recognition, and encourage people to visit the landing page to learn more about the coffee.

Target Audience

Morning Bell Coffee Roasters primarily targets college students ages 18–24 in Ames, Iowa, especially Iowa State University students who enjoy coffee, need convenient places to study or socialize, and are looking for quality coffee at an affordable price. The secondary audience includes young professionals and local residents who value freshly roasted coffee, a welcoming café atmosphere, and community events. The campaign uses engaging digital ads, appealing visuals, and a memorable message to encourage these audiences to discover the brand.

Campaign Hook

“Fresh Roasted. Made for Your Morning.”

This message connects Morning Bell Coffee Roasters with the everyday coffee routine. It introduces the brand in a simple, memorable way and encourages people to explore what it offers.

Call to Action

Discover Our Coffee encourages viewers to click an ad and visit the landing page to learn more about Morning Bell Coffee Roasters.

Ad Placements

The campaign includes four digital display formats: Medium Rectangle (300 × 250 px), Leaderboard (728 × 90 px), Wide Skyscraper (160 × 600 px), and Half Page (300 × 600 px). The leaderboard sits across the top of the creative gallery, while the smaller rectangle appears in the main content area. The tall formats are displayed in a sidebar to demonstrate how they could fit alongside website content. Each ad links to the same landing page.

Measuring the Campaign

Financial Estimates

A proposed one-month budget and industry benchmarks help estimate the potential reach and engagement of the campaign.

Budget and Industry Benchmarks

Our campaign will use a proposed budget of $250 for one month to increase brand awareness for Morning Bell Coffee Roasters. Since coffee-specific display advertising benchmarks were not available, broader restaurant and food industry benchmarks are used for planning.

*The CPM is calculated using the industry's CTR and CPC, rather than being directly reported by the source. These are planning estimates based on broader restaurant and food industry benchmarks, not actual Morning Bell campaign results.

Expected Campaign Outcomes

With a $250 budget, Morning Bell could generate approximately 49,800 ad impressions and 60 clicks. Because the primary goal is brand awareness, the main focus will be increasing the number of people who see and recognize the brand. Clicks and website visits will be secondary measures of engagement.

How Success Will Be Measured

Campaign performance can be evaluated by tracking impressions, click-through rate, cost per click, and landing-page visits. These metrics can help determine whether the ads are reaching college students and building awareness of Morning Bell Coffee Roasters. Conversion rate is not estimated because a conversion benchmark was not available for this campaign.
